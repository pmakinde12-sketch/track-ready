Did you know? Under the current COSS handbook (HB7 Issue 10, in force since 6 December 2025), lookout warning can only be used if approaching trains are 25 mph or less, it's planned in advance and authorised at director level. A lot of people on track still have the old rules in their heads. Track Ready keeps them current.

Questions

Is this official training?

No. Track Ready is a revision and practice tool. It doesn't replace your accredited course, assessment or your COSS briefing. Always follow the current Rule Book handbooks.

Where do the answers come from?

Every question is written in our own words from the current Rule Book handbooks, and each answer shows the handbook, issue and section, so you can check it against your own copy.

How do you keep up with rule changes?

The Rule Book is updated every June and December. We review the briefing leaflet each time, update the questions and scenarios, and add a plain-English "what's changed" summary.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It runs in the browser on any phone, tablet or laptop. Nothing to install.

Track Ready is an independent revision tool. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Network Rail or RSSB. The Rule Book is published by RSSB. Questions are original and reference the handbooks.
